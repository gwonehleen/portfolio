Mobile Platform

What

  • Work with a team to design and manufacture a detachable, height-adjustable platform to assist a little person in safely transitioning into and out of a wheelchair
  • Consistently communicate with project partner to assess needs

How?

  • Led 5+ stages of rapid prototyping to develop a new wheelchair mount
  • Fabricated custom aluminum mounts using CNC machining
  • Performed hands-on alignment and strength testing
  • Modified top bar through milling

Results

  • We received positive feedback from our project partner, who successfully used our platform during a trip
  • The platform demonstrated durability and reliability throughout airport travel
